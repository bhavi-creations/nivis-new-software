Product stock and GST dropdown update

Product quantity means total stock received, including units already sold.
Available = total received - quantities on issued, non-cancelled invoices.
Quotation/order/draft saves do not reserve or consume stock. Issue Invoice records
the sale; cancellation restores availability. Existing issued invoices count too.
To restock, increase total quantity received. Products with sales cannot be deleted
or have their stock unit changed; mark them inactive when necessary.

Products shows total, sold, available and availability status. Sold opens customer,
quantity, invoice date, issue timestamp and invoice links. Product editing previews
available quantity, and order/quotation/invoice entry shows current available stock.
Stock is calculated from database invoices; no stock reset or new migration needed.
Issuing locks product rows and checks the combined quantities for duplicate lines,
preventing concurrent invoices from overselling. KG/GRAM and LITER/ML conversions
are supported. Other differing units require correction before issuing.
Legacy incompatible units or sales exceeding configured total stock are flagged.

GST dropdown displays percentage, treatment (for non-taxable rates), and HSN scope.
Equivalent active options are grouped; editing preserves the product's existing
rate ID. Existing configured rates and invoice tax snapshots are not rewritten.
The dropdown is not a declaration that every configured percentage applies to
every cosmetic. Select the rate applicable to the particular goods and HSN.
